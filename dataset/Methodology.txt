INDIA JOB MAP — LIVING COST DATASET — 16 October 2026

Coverage
- CITY file: 5,050 LGD urban local body (ULB) records from 23 Sep 2026 LGD-derived snapshot; these include municipalities, town panchayats, corporations and similar areas, not every settlement or village. Some metropolitan areas appear as multiple ULBs. City and ULB are not interchangeable.
- DISTRICT file: 784 district records from previous LGD 23 Sep 2026 dataset. District values are proxies, not a surveyed district headquarters city rate.

MODEL WARNING
All monthly INR costs are ESTIMATES, not observed city-by-city market prices. The 2026 Numbeo indexes are reference signals for selected major cities; converting an index into a monthly rupee budget is our model. For other locations, type-of-ULB and state adjustments are heuristic assumptions, not officially established cost-of-living indices. Small-area estimates may be materially inaccurate.

Definition of 'normal' or modest private rental budget
Single: one person renting a 1-bedroom flat OUTSIDE the city centre, with ordinary groceries/food, utilities, transport and miscellaneous expenses.
Family3: 3 people renting an estimated 2-bedroom flat outside city centre with economies of scale. No childcare/private school fees, major healthcare events, savings, loan payments or taxes in expenses.
Rupee amounts are estimates as of Oct 2026, rounded to nearest ₹100.

Formula assumptions (not statistical observations)
National single person non-rent budget = Rs 27,300 /month, decomposed as food 10,500; utilities 3,500; transport 3,500; other 9,800. Source for Rs 27,300 national anchor: India Today, 18 March 2026, citing Numbeo.
National 1BHK outside-centre benchmark Rs 8,822 (Numbeo country page June 2026).
Single non-rent components = component_anchor * model_cost_index / 20.0
Single 1BHK rent = 8822 * model_rent_index / 5.0
Family 3 2BHK rent = single 1BHK estimate * 1.60
Family 3 food = single food * 2.40; utilities = single utilities * 1.60; transport = single transport * 2.00; other = single other * 1.75
All components individually rounded to nearest Rs 100; totals sum rounded components.
Low/high plausible planning bands = 75% /125% of model total, not statistically derived confidence intervals.
Official ULB type codes are kept intact but proxy tiers are modeling categories.

Map/geolocation precautions
- historical_city_point_2011 = actual published city coordinate from an older 2011 Census >=100k population source. Not a current city boundary geometry.
- approx_city_center_major_city = manually populated major city centre coordinates.
- district_centroid_proxy_not_city_coordinates = approximate DISTRICT polygon centre, NOT the city centre (rough display fallback only).
- not_available = latitude and longitude intentionally left blank. Do not display at [0,0], and do not geocode to state centre without additional evidence.
Not all 5,050 ULBs have city coordinates; city name + state code is the most useful stable key.
